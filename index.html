What to notice

  1. 1Each column is one full adder — the same box you built, copied.Abstraction lets us build a 4-bit adder without redrawing 20 gates.
  2. 2The carry ripples from right to left.Bit 3 can't settle until bits 0, 1 and 2 have. More bits = slower adder.
  3. 3Bit 0 has a carry-in of 0.So a half adder would do there — why does the rest need a full adder?
  4. 4Try 9 + 8 in 4 bits.The final carry out shows the answer no longer fits: overflow.
